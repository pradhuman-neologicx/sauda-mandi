What is a Trading Firm & What is its Purpose?

Core Business Entity architecture powering multi-firm Mandi trading

What is a Trading Firm?

In this Mandi ERP, a Trading Firm (Client Firm) represents your official registered business entity, proprietorship, or partnership trading firm (e.g., Desert Fresh Traders, Rajasthan Agro). If you operate multiple trading entities under one brokerage or family group, each entity is managed as a distinct Firm.

Core Purpose & Usage in ERP
  • Trade Bookings: Every Purchase & Sale Sauda is executed on behalf of a specific Firm.
  • Inventory Segregation: Godown stock and warehouse lots belong to specific Firms.
  • Independent Accounts & Ledger: Separate GST invoicing, receivables, and payables for each firm.
  • Automated Dispatch: Official invoices and receipts are dispatched via the Firm's registered WhatsApp & Email.
